# OpenPath Growth

Context

Build a polished, responsive landing page for OpenWays, a Singapore-based business development and growth advisory.

OpenWays helps companies create and execute clearer paths to growth through business development, strategic partnerships, market expansion, and change-management advisory.

The site must communicate within 30 seconds:

What OpenWays does

Who it helps

What kind of support is available

Why someone should speak with OpenWays

This is not a startup/SaaS landing page, a corporate consulting website, or a lead-generation template.

The feeling should be:
warm, intelligent, commercially sharp, international, approachable and memorable.

Avoid anything that looks overly corporate, cold, generic, techy, or like a management-consulting template.

Primary Goal

The main conversion is:

Start a conversation with OpenWays

Use one primary CTA throughout the site. It can appear more than once, but every occurrence must lead to the same contact action.

Recommended CTA:

Let's talk

Do not add competing actions such as:

Book a demo

Join a newsletter

Download a guide

Sign up

Learn more buttons on every section

Brand Direction

Use the uploaded OpenWays logo as the visual starting point.

Brand personality

Open

Human

Strategic

International

Curious

Commercial

Confident without being aggressive

Visual direction

The design should subtly communicate the idea of:
opening paths, creating connections, movement, access and new possibilities.

Do this through the layout itself rather than literal illustrations of doors.

Use:

flowing visual transitions

open negative space

subtle paths or directional lines

overlapping sections

asymmetric composition

gentle curves

editorial layouts

elements that visually lead the eye down the page

Do NOT use

grids of identical square cards

repetitive boxes

generic consulting icons

stock photos of handshakes

skyscraper/business-team stock photography

excessive gradients

dark navy corporate backgrounds

pure white or very pale sterile cream backgrounds

overly rounded SaaS components

floating dashboard graphics

excessive animation

The site should feel designed rather than assembled from UI components.

Color

Derive the palette primarily from the OpenWays logo.

Use a sophisticated combination of:

medium/muted blue

softer sky blue

warm off-white / sand

darker blue-grey for text

Avoid extremely dark navy and stark white.

Use accent colors sparingly.

Typography

Use Cormorant Garamond for important headlines or selected editorial moments.

Pair it with a clean, highly readable sans-serif font for body copy and navigation.

Typography should be expressive enough to make the site memorable but still professional.

Use large editorial headlines, clear hierarchy and generous spacing.

Do not make every headline oversized.

Page Structure

1. Navigation

Minimal navigation.

Left:
OpenWays logo

Right:

Services

Who we work with

About

Contact

CTA:
Let's talk

Keep navigation light and spacious.

2. Hero

The hero should immediately explain the business.

Use the headline:

Open ways to growth.

Supporting copy:

OpenWays helps companies turn growth ambitions into commercial opportunities through business development, strategic partnerships and market expansion.

Add a short supporting sentence:

From finding the right opportunities and partners to entering new markets and strengthening commercial strategy, we help companies work out where to go next — and how to get there.

Primary CTA:

Let's talk

The hero should feel open and inviting rather than like a conventional consulting homepage.

Avoid putting the entire hero inside a rectangular container.

Create a visual sense of a path opening or moving forward through typography, spacing and composition.

3. Make the Visitor Recognise Their Situation

Create a visually engaging transition into:

Growth is rarely just about finding more customers.

Supporting copy:

Companies often reach a point where the opportunity is there, but the next move is less obvious.

Show situations such as:

You are entering a new market and need to understand where you fit.

Meetings and introductions are happening, but they are not turning into opportunities.

You need stronger partnerships, channels or local connections.

Your business development activity lacks structure or clear priorities.

Growth requires internal change, but you need an outside strategic perspective.

You know where you want to grow, but not yet the best route to get there.

Do not show these as six identical cards.

Use an editorial or flowing layout that lets visitors naturally recognise themselves.

4. Services

Headline:

How OpenWays can help

The services must be extremely easy to understand.

Avoid vague consulting terminology.

Business Development

Help companies build a more deliberate commercial growth process.

Include:

Commercial strategy planning and opportunity mapping

Lead generation and pipeline development

Sales process strategy and meeting support

Decision-maker access and local representation

Strategic Partnerships

Help companies identify and build partnerships that have a clear commercial purpose.

Include:

Identify and prioritise the right potential partners

Define what each partnership should achieve

Develop the partnership structure and mutual value

Support introductions, negotiations and next steps

Build a clear process for managing partnership opportunities

Market Entry & Expansion

Help companies understand and navigate unfamiliar markets, particularly Singapore and the wider APAC region.

Include:

Market and opportunity assessment

Entry strategy and commercial priorities

Local positioning and offer adaptation

Customer, partner and stakeholder mapping

Local introductions and representation

Market feedback before committing significant resources

Growth & Commercial Advisory

Provide an external strategic perspective when a company needs to sharpen its approach.

Include:

Identify commercial gaps and missed opportunities

Review growth priorities and go-to-market choices

Define practical KPIs and ways to measure progress

Challenge assumptions and pressure-test decisions

Turn broad growth ambitions into concrete priorities

Change Management Advisory

Support leadership teams when growth or strategic decisions require internal change.

This is an advisory service, not outsourced internal communications or implementation.

Include:

Assess how a proposed change may affect teams and stakeholders

Identify adoption risks and potential resistance

Advise on communication and stakeholder-alignment approaches

Define indicators to track adoption and progress

Recommend adjustments as implementation develops

5. Who We Work With

Headline:

Companies looking to:

Include:

Build a stronger business-development pipeline

Develop strategic partnerships

Enter Singapore or expand across APAC

Turn introductions and meetings into commercial opportunities

Create a clearer growth strategy

Navigate growth that requires internal change

Build local relationships without immediately building a full local team

Make clear that OpenWays can work with:

Startups

Growing SMEs

International companies entering Singapore/APAC

Do not make these feel like rigid packages or imply that every client needs the same engagement.

6. How We Work

Keep this concise.

Headline:

Built around the opportunity, not a fixed playbook.

Copy:

Every company reaches growth differently. OpenWays starts by understanding the commercial objective, the market and the obstacles in the way. From there, we define where external support can create the most value.

Show a simple progression:

Understand → Prioritise → Connect → Move forward

This should feel like a visual journey rather than four rectangular process cards.

Add:

Engagements can range from focused advisory sessions to ongoing business-development, partnership or market-entry support.

7. Why OpenWays

Avoid generic claims such as:

Tailored solutions

Results driven

Innovative thinking

Trusted partner

End-to-end solutions

Instead communicate concrete differentiation.

Headline:

Commercial thinking with a stakeholder perspective.

Copy around these ideas:

OpenWays combines business-development thinking with experience working across complex stakeholder environments.

That means looking beyond a simple sales funnel to understand:

who influences a decision

which relationships matter

where interests align

what may block progress

and what needs to happen to move an opportunity forward

Emphasise the ability to connect strategy, relationships and execution.

Keep this section concise and credible.

Do not invent client numbers, success rates, logos, testimonials or credentials.

8. About

Headline:

About OpenWays

OpenWays was founded by Liad Kaikov in Singapore.

Write a concise introduction positioning Liad's background around:

business development

partnerships

stakeholder engagement

international environments

public-policy and cross-sector experience

Singapore/APAC market exposure

Do not write a long biography.

Do not exaggerate experience or invent achievements.

The purpose of this section is to show visitors that there is an experienced person behind the business, rather than making OpenWays feel like an anonymous consultancy.

Leave room for a professional portrait.

9. Final CTA

This section can be more visually distinctive and warm.

Headline:

Let's open the way to your next stage of growth.

Supporting text:

If you're exploring a new market, partnership or commercial opportunity — or simply trying to work out the smartest next move — let's talk.

CTA:

Let's talk

Provide contact options for:

email

LinkedIn

Keep it simple.

Footer

OpenWays logo

Singapore

Minimal links:

LinkedIn

Email

Small copyright line.

Do not clutter the footer.

Interaction & UX

The page must be fully responsive.

Prioritise:

desktop

mobile

tablet

Use subtle animation only where it supports the concept of movement and opening paths.

Possible effects:

gentle reveal on scroll

lines/path elements progressing as the visitor scrolls

subtle text transitions

slight image movement

No excessive parallax or distracting effects.

Ensure excellent mobile readability.

Do not make mobile feel like stacked desktop cards.

Contact Form

If a contact form is included, keep it short.

Fields:

Name

Company

Email

What are you looking to achieve?

CTA:
Let's talk

Use inline validation.

After successful submission, replace the form with a clean inline confirmation.

Do not use browser alerts or redirect users.

SEO

Build proper semantic HTML.

Suggested title:

OpenWays | Business Development, Partnerships & Market Expansion

Suggested meta description:

OpenWays helps companies grow through business development, strategic partnerships, market expansion and commercial advisory in Singapore and across APAC.

Use one H1 only.

Add appropriate OpenGraph metadata.

Critical Design Rules

Before considering the page finished, check that:

A new visitor understands what OpenWays does within 30 seconds.

The design does not look like a generic consulting template.

The page does not rely on grids of rectangular cards.

Every section has a clear purpose.

There is enough whitespace to keep the page calm and premium.

Business Development and Partnerships clearly describe actual activities rather than consulting jargon.

There are no invented testimonials, clients, statistics or claims.

There is only one conversion goal: starting a conversation.

Repeated CTAs all lead to the same contact action.

The design feels warm and approachable while still being credible for larger international companies.

The site works equally well for a startup, growing SME or foreign company evaluating Singapore/APAC.

The visual identity conveys movement, access, connections and opening new paths without using obvious door graphics.

Build Order

Make the design responsive.

Add subtle interactions and animations.

Add SEO metadata.

Run a final visual pass specifically looking for generic SaaS/consulting patterns and replace them with more editorial, fluid compositions.
