Why change management matters today

Keep the business running while you adapt

Nowadays, change is accelerating. Organisations are processing more information and navigating greater complexity and uncertainty. As the external environment moves faster, the ability to adapt well becomes a real advantage.

OpenWays helps leaders, partners and operational teams work out how change should function in practice. We stay close through implementation, helping resolve questions, adjust the approach and support people as new ways of working take hold.

Where change gets stuck, and how OpenWays helps

When three sensible priorities become a barrier

A hypothetical rollout, but a familiar one.

The project sponsor

Wants every step tracked in the new system, so progress can be reported.

The vendor

Has to go live on the agreed date, so unusual cases are left for later.

The operations team

Has to keep serving customers, with little time to learn the new steps.

The common outcome

The system went live on time. The old spreadsheets never left.

The new process missed some everyday cases, so teams kept their own workarounds. The sponsor’s reports show part of the picture, and the vendor sees lower usage than planned.

    Workarounds appear

    Cases the process missed are handled in team spreadsheets.

    Spreadsheets back

  3. Month 3

    Two ways of working

From competing priorities to workable decisions

OpenWays helps align stakeholders and create a shared understanding of what needs to change, who owns the next decision, and how the new workflow will work in practice.

How OpenWays answers each challenge

  • As a third party, OpenWays provides clarity, ensures a common understanding of the different processes and increases trust.

    Result: One shared picture of priorities, owners and progress.

  • OpenWays builds contacts and trust inside the organisation, discovers expectations and barriers, raises them on the decision makers’ agenda and advises on the right way to address them.

    Result: Exceptions and potential barriers are raised before go-live and become part of the design.

  • Together we build a practical change plan with clear owners and measures of progress. OpenWays stays close as it rolls out, adjusting the approach as new ways of working take hold.

    Result: A workflow that works on an ordinary day, with progress everyone can see.

The OpenWays outcome

By go-live, the team’s workarounds were already part of the new process.

Team leads raised the cases the design missed before launch, and the project team built them in or agreed clear interim steps. The work stays in one system, and everyone sees the same picture.

    Go-live on time

  3. Month 3
