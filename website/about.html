About

My work has always been about helping people move forward together.

I have worked across public policy, politics and technology adoption. I have led multicultural teams, aligned stakeholders with different priorities and supported people as new ways of working were introduced.

These experiences taught me to listen across roles, connect strategic objectives with daily work and stay close enough to implementation to see what needs attention.

I founded OpenWays to bring that approach to organisations going through change: practical, collaborative and grounded in the realities of the people doing the work.
